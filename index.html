Questions

Before you order

How does payment work?

You place the order, then send the amount by Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer, PayPal or Wise. Add your payment reference on the order page. Work starts once the seller confirms the payment.

Which payment method should I pick?

If you are in Pakistan, Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer let you pay in rupees. From anywhere else, PayPal, Wise or a card link let you pay in dollars.

Can I pay in rupees?

Yes. Switch the currency at the top of the page. Rupee prices follow a fixed exchange rate shown above the packages, so the amount you see is the amount you pay.

How do I know my payment arrived?

After you add your payment reference, your order moves to Payment submitted. When the seller confirms it, the status changes to Paid and the progress bar moves forward.

How long until my website is ready?

Each package lists its delivery time, and checkout shows the exact ready-by date. The Rush extra shortens delivery to 3 days. The clock starts when the seller has your payment and your details.

What do I need to send you?

Your logo, the text for each page and your photos. Each package lists exactly what you provide under Full details. If you have no logo, add the Logo extra.

Do you include a domain and hosting?

The Domain and hosting setup extra covers buying a domain and connecting it to your site. The domain and hosting fees are paid to the provider, not to the seller.

Will my site work on phones?

Yes. Every package uses a mobile-first design, so the layout is built for phones first and then scaled up.

What if I want changes?

Every package includes revisions, and the number is listed in the comparison table. Extra changes beyond that can be quoted separately.

Can I ask for something not listed?

Yes. Use the Contact form to describe it and the seller replies with a quote in dollars and rupees.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once the order is delivered and paid, the site and its content belong to you.

What if I change my mind?

Tell the seller before work starts and the order can be cancelled. Once the build has begun, ask the seller about a partial refund.
